LEO app (test copy)
1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your Mac.
2. Open  hq-k7m2/index.html  (double-click). Sign in with YOUR admin email and password.
3. Add a client (use a test name and one of your own emails). A temporary password shows once.
4. Open  owner.html  in a new tab and sign in with that client's email and the temporary password.
5. Open  index.html?shop=PASTE-THE-SHOP-ID  to see that shop's website (the shop ID is in the address when you press "Open their space").


GOING ONLINE (Cloudflare Pages, free)
1. Make a free account at cloudflare.com (you do this yourself).
2. Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Upload assets. Drag this whole folder in. Name it, Deploy.
3. You get an address like leo-xxxx.pages.dev. Admin: that address + /hq-k7m2/  Owner login: + /owner.html
4. For a client's own domain: in Cloudflare Pages > Custom domains, add it, then type the same domain in the
   "Website domain" box on that client's row in your admin page. The site shows that client's website.
5. The automation timer already runs on its own on the server every 10 minutes (no need to keep a page open).


CUSTOM CLIENT WEBSITE (designed just for one client)
1. Make a folder for them:  sites/their-name/index.html   (sites/northline is a finished example to copy)
2. In that page, after the config.js line, add:  <script>LEO.shopId = 'THEIR-SHOP-ID'</script>
   (the shop ID is in the "Preview website" link on their row in your admin page: the part after ?shop=)
3. The page loads connect.js, which gives it:  LeoConnect.load()  (their name, hours, services, team from their dashboard)
                                               LeoConnect.book()  (a booking request that lands in their dashboard)
                                               LeoConnect.ownerUrl()  (the shared owner login link)
4. Their site address:  https://abulawi-o.github.io/shopmate/sites/their-name/   (or their own domain later)
5. Their dashboard and AI system need nothing extra. Same shop, same data.
