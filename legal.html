Terms for shop owners

These terms are between you (the shop) and your provider (the provider). By signing in you agree to them.

What you get

  • A private control panel for your shop, and, if included in your plan, a website and the AI automation system (reminders, review requests, missed-call text-back, inquiry follow-up and win-back messages).
  • Your plan, what it includes and your next payment date are set by the provider and shown to you on request.

Your responsibilities

  • Only add customers' phone numbers and emails if the customer has agreed to be contacted, or you have a legitimate existing relationship with them under Canada's anti-spam law (CASL). Tick the consent box only when that is true.
  • Every message sent for you includes your business name, address and a way to stop (reply STOP). Customers who stop are never messaged again. Do not try to get around this.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your login.
  • Keep your shop details (hours, services, prices) accurate.

Messages and practice mode

  • Messages are written by the system and wait for your OK before they go out. The system never books appointments by itself: you accept or decline each request.
  • While a shop is in practice mode, nothing is actually sent to customers.

Payments and pausing

  • Your plan is billed on the dates agreed with the provider. If a payment is overdue the provider may pause the website or the automation system until it is paid. Your data is kept while paused.
  • You can ask to cancel at any time. After cancellation your data is deleted on request.

Availability and liability

  • We work hard to keep the service running, but it is provided as is, with no promise it will be uninterrupted or error free. Check your appointments and messages.
  • To the extent the law allows, the provider is not responsible for lost bookings, lost income or indirect losses. Our total responsibility is limited to what you paid for the service in the last 3 months.
  • These terms may be updated. Continuing to use the service means you accept the new version. Ontario law applies.

Privacy

We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and anti-spam law (CASL). Short version: we only keep what the service needs, only the shop sees its own customers, and we never sell data.

What we collect

  • Shop owners: name, email, shop details and when you last signed in.
  • Customers of a shop: name, phone number, the appointment or request, and a record of whether they agreed to be contacted (this record is kept as proof of consent).

Why

  • To run bookings and send the reminders and follow-ups the shop has set up, and to keep proof of consent.

Who can see it

  • Each shop sees only its own customers. The provider can open a shop's space only to set up or fix things, and each time is logged. Data is stored with a secure cloud database provider. We do not sell or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • While the shop is a client. After that, on request or within a reasonable time, we delete it. Consent records may be kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Contact the provider. If a shop holds your information, you can also ask the shop directly.

For customers of a shop

  • You only get messages if you agreed, or you recently did business or asked a question, and the shop is allowed to contact you under CASL.
  • Every message says who it is from and how to stop. Reply STOP at any time and you will not be messaged again.
  • Your details are used only for your booking and the shop's reminders. To see or delete them, ask the shop or contact the provider.
