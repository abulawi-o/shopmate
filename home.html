How it works

We set it up and run it. You keep doing what you do best.

1

Your custom website

Built for your shop, with online booking requests that land in your own panel.

2

Texts that run themselves

Missed calls, reminders and review requests are written for you. You approve the wording once.

3

You stay in control

You set the wording and the rules. Customers who reply STOP are never texted again.

What's included

One simple setup instead of juggling several apps.

Missed-call text-back

A friendly text goes to people you could not answer, with a link to book.

Appointment reminders

A text the day before and again on the day, so fewer people forget.

Review requests

After a visit, every customer gets a friendly text with a link to leave a Google review.

Change or cancel link

Customers move or cancel their own appointment from the reminder. You confirm the new time.

Client profiles

Visit history, usual service and your private notes for every regular.

Win-back messages

A note to customers you have not seen in a while, with wording you approve first.
