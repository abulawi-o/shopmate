How to use your control panel

A five-minute guide. Everything here is on the left menu.

Signing in

  • Go to your login link and use the email and password your provider gave you.
  • Tick Remember my email so it is filled in next time, and Keep me signed in so you stay signed in on your own phone or computer. Untick it on a shared computer.
  • Change your password any time under My login. Forgot it? Press "Forgot password?" or ask your provider.

Overview

Your day at a glance: today's appointments, new requests, messages waiting for you and a chart of the last 8 weeks.

Requests

When someone books on your website it lands here. Press Accept to confirm or Decline. The system never books by itself. Only accepted appointments get reminders.

Appointments

See the day, move between days and add an appointment for someone who called or walked in. Tick the consent box only if the customer agreed to get text reminders. Without it, no texts are sent to them.

Automations

  • The system writes messages for you: reminders, review requests, missed-call text-backs, inquiry follow-ups and win-back messages.
  • Under Waiting for your OK you read each one and approve or skip it.
  • Customers who reply STOP, or who never agreed, are blocked automatically. You'll see those under "Stopped by the rules".

Customers

Everyone with an accepted appointment. Log a missed call or an inquiry here and the system prepares the follow-up.

Team and staff

Add your barbers and the days they work. Customers on your website only see barbers who are working that day. Mark someone as away when they are off.

Website

Change your tagline, phone, address, opening hours, services, prices and social links. Changes show on your website right away.

Need help?

Contact your provider. See also Terms and privacy.
